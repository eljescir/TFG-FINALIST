Meet the owner

Passion turned into standard motorcycle service.

The Feng's Garage started the way many good garages do: as a passion and a hobby. Fixing motorcycles was something he loved long before it became a business.

He used to work as a restaurant manager. Leaving that job was never part of the plan, but one day he went in and handed over his resignation letter. Today, he has spent about 6 years in the motorcycle industry.

The Kymco Like is the bike he loves, and the riding group he became part of. That is why TFG focuses on it: every job is on a bike he knows inside and out.
